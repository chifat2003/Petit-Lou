About Us

French Café Brasserie in
Amsterdam Centrum

Petit Lou is more than just a café – it's a slice of Paris in the heart of Amsterdam.

We serve breakfast, brunch, lunch, and dinner with a distinctly French approach. Whether you're stopping for a morning coffee and croissant, a leisurely lunch, or pre-theater dining before a show at Carré, we welcome you with open arms.
