# Petit Lou Amsterdam - Landing Page

A personalized landing page for cold outreach to Petit Lou Amsterdam, a French café brasserie in central Amsterdam.

## 🎯 Purpose

This landing page demonstrates genuine research and understanding of Petit Lou's business by highlighting specific, actionable improvements to their current website (petitlouamsterdam.nl).

## 📊 Business Overview

- **Type**: French café brasserie
- **Location**: Weesperstraat 101A, Amsterdam (near Carré & Stopera)
- **Services**: Breakfast, brunch, lunch, dinner, drinks
- **Current Website Rating**: 6/10
- **Key Opportunity**: Website redesign and optimization

## 🔍 Research-Based Approach

The landing page is built around **5 specific findings** from analyzing their actual website:

1. **Homepage doesn't sell strongly** - Needs impactful hero section
2. **Weak reservation CTA** - Not prominent enough for a restaurant
3. **Menu needs modernization** - Could be more engaging and mobile-friendly
4. **Missing local SEO** - Not capitalizing on proximity to cultural venues
5. **Mobile experience** - Needs optimization
